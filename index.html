What is Fantasy Premier League?

Fantasy Premier League (FPL) is the ultimate tactical game for football fans worldwide. With a budget of £100m, you step into the shoes of a manager to build your dream 15-player squad from the English Premier League.

Every gameweek, your players earn points based on their real-life performances on the pitch—scoring goals, providing assists, keeping clean sheets, or saving penalties.
